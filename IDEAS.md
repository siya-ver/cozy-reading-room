# Ideas

A running list for the reading room. Add freely, build slowly.

## Shipped
- Coffee table pile, aging paper, ribbon bookmark, mug ring for favorites
- Window time of day, lamp toggle
- Chrome extension with highlight + note
- Newspaper editions: every 10 finished articles bind into an issue you edit
- The reader by the lamp (nameable)
- Treehouse framing with locked rooms

## Up next (rough priority)
- **Collectibles + decorating.** Finishing an article sometimes drops a small object (a mug, a plant cutting, a record, a postcard). Cash them in to decorate the nook. Focus Friends energy, no pressure.
- **Unlock the treehouse.** Rooms beyond the nook open at read-count milestones. Candidates: the Print Shop (where issues get bound and printed), the Darkroom (lead images from saved articles as photos on a line), the Observatory (a map of where your sources are from), the Archive (search everything).
- **When the rack is full.** Instead of infinite scroll, you buy a second rack or a bigger table with collectibles. Scarcity makes the room feel like a room.
- **Wrapped.** A recap you can generate any time: articles read, words read, top sources, your most-used margin words, the issue you spent longest on, a "your year in headlines" page. Rendered as a special edition of your paper.

## Newspaper
- Let the editor drag stories to reorder the front page
- Headline rewrite: tap a headline and write your own
- Print or export an issue as PDF
- Weekly "Sunday edition" that auto-compiles the week's saves even if under 10
- Red string: an LLM finds connections between articles in an issue and draws them on a corkboard view

## Character
- Reading animations: she picks up the paper when you open one, stamps the issue when it binds
- Outfits and headphones as collectibles
- A cat that eventually naps on the pile

## Small touches
- Page-rustle sound on open, with a mute toggle
- Rain on the window on real rainy days (weather API)
- Keyboard shortcut to save from the extension
- Reading streaks shown as a calendar on the wall
- Tags as colored tabs sticking out of papers
